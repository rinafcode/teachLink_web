# Code of Conduct Enforcement Ladder

## Purpose

This document defines the graduated steps the project takes when a
breach of the TeachLink Web Code of Conduct is confirmed. It sets
the criteria for each step and who may apply it, so enforcement is
proportionate, predictable, and consistent from one case to the
next.

## Scope

This ladder applies to breaches of `Governance/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`
confirmed through the process in `Governance/COC_REPORTING.md`.

It does not cover moderation of individual comments, which follows
`Governance/policies/MODERATION.md`, nor the appeal of a decision,
which follows `Governance/COC_APPEALS.md`.

## Enforcement Steps

The steps are graduated. Enforcement normally starts at the lowest
step that fits the behavior, and moves up when behavior repeats or
worsens. A severe breach may start at any step.

### 1. Correction

- **Criteria:** a first, minor breach, such as inappropriate language
  or unprofessional behavior, where no one was targeted or harmed.
- **Action:** a private written note explaining what was wrong and
  what is expected. A public apology may be requested.
- **Applied by:** any maintainer.

### 2. Warning

- **Criteria:** a repeated minor breach after a correction, or a
  single breach that caused harm to another person.
- **Action:** a formal written warning with the consequences of
  further breaches. No contact with the people involved for a stated
  period.
- **Applied by:** any maintainer, with a second maintainer informed.

### 3. Temporary Suspension

- **Criteria:** a serious breach, or a breach after a warning,
  including sustained inappropriate behavior.
- **Action:** a temporary ban from all project spaces for a stated
  period, normally 7 to 90 days. No interaction with the community,
  including through other accounts.
- **Applied by:** two maintainers in agreement.

### 4. Permanent Ban

- **Criteria:** a pattern of breaches after a temporary suspension,
  or a severe breach such as harassment, a threat of violence, or
  doxxing.
- **Action:** a permanent ban from all project spaces.
- **Applied by:** a majority of active maintainers.

## Who Applies Enforcement

- **Maintainers apply enforcement**, as set out in
  `Governance/roles/MAINTAINER.md`, at the authority each step names.
- **Conflicts of interest are declared.** A maintainer who is
  involved in the matter, or has a personal stake, steps aside under
  `Governance/policies/CONFLICT_OF_INTEREST.md`.
- **Maintainers are not exempt.** When the reported person is a
  maintainer, the decision is taken by the other maintainers, or by
  project leadership when too few remain.
- **Interim action is allowed.** Any maintainer may take immediate
  interim action, such as hiding content or a temporary pause,
  to stop ongoing harm while a decision is made.

## Decision Record

Every enforcement decision is recorded in the private conduct record
with the step applied, the criteria met, the reasoning, and who
applied it. The affected person receives the decision in writing,
with the reason and the appeal route in `Governance/COC_APPEALS.md`.

## Ownership and Review

- Maintainers own this ladder and are accountable for applying it
  consistently.
- Changes to this ladder are proposed in a pull request that touches
  only the Governance/ folder.
- This governance document is versioned with the repository; it
  describes the process the project actually follows.

## Success

This ladder succeeds when similar breaches receive similar outcomes,
when every decision is recorded with its reasoning, and when most
breaches are resolved at the lowest step.

## Regression Tests

Coverage is provided by `Governance/COC_ENFORCEMENT.test.ts`, which
pins the enforcement guarantees in this document.
