# TeachLink Web Brand Usage Guidelines

This document outlines the rules for using the TeachLink Web brand, including our logo and name. Adherence to these guidelines is crucial for maintaining a consistent and recognizable brand identity.

## Logo Usage

### Do's

- Use the official TeachLink Web logo for all branding purposes.
- Maintain the logo's aspect ratio and colors.
- Ensure there is sufficient clear space around the logo.

### Don'ts

- Do not alter, rotate, or modify the logo in any way.
- Do not use the logo in a way that suggests endorsement or sponsorship without permission.
- Do not use the logo on any materials that violate our terms of service.

## Name Usage

### Do's

- Use the full name "TeachLink Web" in the first mention.
- "TeachLink" can be used in subsequent mentions.

### Don'ts

- Do not abbreviate or alter the name "TeachLink Web."
- Do not use "TeachLink" in a way that could be confused with other products or services.

## Asset Location

All official brand assets, including logos and color palettes, are located in the `/assets/brand` directory of this repository.

## Questions

If you have any questions about these guidelines, please open an issue in the repository.
