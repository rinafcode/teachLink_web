# Conflict of Interest Policy

This document defines what constitutes a conflict of interest for TeachLink Web contributors and maintainers, and outlines the process for disclosure and recusal.

## What Constitutes a Conflict of Interest

A conflict of interest may arise when a contributor or maintainer has a personal interest that could potentially influence their decisions or actions related to the project. This includes, but is not limited to:

- Financial interests in competing or partner organizations.
- Personal relationships with individuals who may be affected by project decisions.
- Employment or advisory roles in organizations that could be seen as having a stake in the project's direction.

## Disclosure Requirement

All contributors and maintainers are required to disclose any potential conflicts of interest to the project's leadership. This can be done privately if necessary.

## Recusal Process

In the event of a conflict of interest, the individual must recuse themselves from any related discussions and decisions. The project leadership will ensure that the recusal is respected and that the decision-making process remains fair and unbiased.
