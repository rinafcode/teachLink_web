# TeachLink Governance: Meeting Cadence & Process

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Scope:** All synchronization calls, core maintainer syncs, and community working groups within the TeachLink ecosystem.

---

## 1. Purpose
To maintain alignment, foster transparent collaboration, and respect the time of distributed contributors across global time zones, this document defines the official meeting schedule, meeting classifications, and attendance expectations for TeachLink projects.

---

## 2. Meeting Schedule & Cadence

* **Core Maintainer Sync:** Bi-weekly on Thursdays at 14:00 UTC. Focused on architectural reviews, roadmap milestone tracking, and cross-repository synchronization.
* **Community Open Office Hours:** Monthly on the first Tuesday at 16:00 UTC. Open to all contributors, developers, and community members for Q&A, proposal discussions, and onboarding support.
* **Ad-Hoc Working Groups:** Scheduled dynamically as needed for specific engineering epics (e.g., smart contract upgrades, security audits) and documented publicly in advance.

---

## 3. Meeting Types & Formats
1. **Synchronous Alignment Meetings:** Real-time video/audio syncs used exclusively for complex architectural debates, consensus building, and blocker resolution.
2. **Async-First Updates:** Routine status updates, sprint progress reports, and non-blocking reviews must be handled asynchronously (governed by `Governance/policies/ASYNC_DECISIONS.md`) rather than consuming live meeting time.

---

## 4. Attendance Expectations
* **Core Maintainers:** Expected to attend at least 70% of scheduled Core Maintainer Syncs per quarter, or provide advance notice of absence with asynchronous status updates.
* **Community Members:** Attendance at Open Office Hours is entirely optional and voluntary.
* **Meeting Records:** Every synchronous meeting must have designated note-taking rotation, with meeting minutes, video recordings (where applicable), and action items published publicly within 24 hours in the Governance repository.
