# Comment Moderation Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how user-generated comments on TeachLink Web are moderated. It
establishes the criteria used to judge comments, the flow for reporting
unacceptable content, and the path for appealing moderation decisions.

## Scope

This policy applies to comments and other user-generated public contributions
published through TeachLink Web, regardless of the page or surface in which they
appear.

## Moderation Criteria

Comments are judged against the following criteria:

- **Relevance**: content should relate to the topic of the page or discussion it
  appears on.
- **Respect**: content must not harass, defame, threaten, or target any person or
  group.
- **Safety**: content must not promote harm, violence, self-harm, or illegal
  activity.
- **Accuracy**: content must not spread materially misleading information that
  could harm users.
- **Compliance**: content must not violate applicable law or the project's
  published community and conduct standards.

A comment that clearly violates any criterion may be removed or hidden, and
repeat or severe violations may lead to further action in line with community
guidelines.

## Reporting Flow

- Any user can report a comment using the reporting mechanism available on the
  comment itself.
- A report must identify the comment and, where possible, the reason for the
  report.
- Reported content is reviewed by a moderator in a timely manner. The reporter
  and, when permitted, the subject of the report receive status feedback on the
  outcome.
- Reports must not be used to retaliate against other users; knowingly false
  reports are themselves a violation.

## Appeal Path

- Users who disagree with a moderation decision may appeal it through the
  documented appeal process.
- An appeal must explain why the original decision should be reconsidered.
- Appeals are reviewed by a party that did not make the original decision.
- Given serious or sustained violations, community conduct and enforcement
  policies may apply in addition to this policy.

## Ownership and Review

- The moderation team owns this policy and reviews its criteria on a regular
  cadence to keep them practical and aligned with community expectations.
- Changes to this policy are proposed in a pull request that touches only the
  `Governance/` folder.

## Success

This policy succeeds when comments are moderated consistently, reported content
is addressed promptly, and users have a clear, fair path to appeal moderation
decisions.
