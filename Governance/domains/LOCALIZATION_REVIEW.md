# Localization Review Process

This document outlines the process for reviewing translations to ensure they are accurate, culturally appropriate, and consistent with our brand voice.

## Translation Review Steps

All translations must be reviewed before they are deployed.

1.  **Initial Translation:** The string is translated by a professional translator or a native speaker.
2.  **Linguistic Review:** A second linguist reviews the translation for accuracy, grammar, and style.
3.  **In-Context Review:** The translation is reviewed within the application to ensure it fits the UI and makes sense in context.
4.  **Approval:** The translation is approved by the localization team.

## Terminology Glossary

A terminology glossary must be used to ensure consistent translation of key terms.

- **Policy:** All translators must use the terminology glossary.
- **Maintenance:** The glossary is maintained by the localization team and is regularly updated.

## Sign-off Per Locale

Each locale must be signed off by a designated locale owner.

- **Responsibility:** The locale owner is responsible for the quality of the translations in their locale.
- **Process:** The locale owner must review and approve all translations for their locale before they can be deployed.

## Regression Testing

All localization-related changes must be accompanied by regression tests to ensure that existing functionality is not broken. This includes tests for translation accuracy, UI layout, and proper rendering of localized content.
