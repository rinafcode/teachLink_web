# SEO and Content Policy

This document outlines the SEO and content policy for TeachLink Web.

## Metadata Requirements

All pages must include the following metadata:

- **Title:** A unique and descriptive title.
- **Description:** A brief summary of the page content.
- **Keywords:** A list of relevant keywords.

## Canonical-URL Rule

Each page must have a canonical URL specified in the `<head>` of the HTML document. This helps to prevent duplicate content issues.

## Content-Quality Checklist

All content must meet the following quality standards:

- **Accuracy:** The information must be accurate and up-to-date.
- **Clarity:** The language must be clear and easy to understand.
- **Originality:** The content must be original and not plagiarized.
- **Relevance:** The content must be relevant to the TeachLink Web project.
