# Privacy Notice

This document outlines the governance process for updating the privacy notice.

## Disclosures

The privacy notice must disclose the following:

- What personal information is collected
- How personal information is used
- With whom personal information is shared
- How personal information is protected
- The rights of individuals regarding their personal information

## Update Process

Any changes to the privacy notice must be reviewed and approved by the legal team.

## Versioning

The privacy notice must be versioned. The version number must be incremented with each change. The version number must be displayed on the privacy notice.
