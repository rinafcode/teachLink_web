# TeachLink Web Core Values

These values describe how TeachLink Web makes decisions, builds software, and
participates in its community.

## Learning

We stay curious, share what we know, and treat mistakes as opportunities to
improve. We value explanations that help others become more capable.

## Inclusion

We make room for different backgrounds, abilities, viewpoints, and levels of
experience. We design participation so that people can contribute with dignity.

## Integrity

We communicate honestly, give credit, protect trust, and take responsibility for
the effects of our decisions. We make important trade-offs visible.

## Craft

We care about quality, clarity, accessibility, security, and maintainability. We
choose simple solutions when they serve people well and improve them deliberately
when needs change.

## Stewardship

We use community time and project resources responsibly. We leave the codebase,
documentation, and relationships stronger for the people who come next.

## How Values Inform Decisions

When priorities conflict, contributors should state the trade-off and explain how
the chosen option best serves users and the long-term health of the project. A
decision that materially affects privacy, safety, access, or trust deserves
additional review and clear documentation.
