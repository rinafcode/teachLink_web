# OKR and Goal Governance Policy for TeachLink

## Overview
This document defines the official Objective and Key Results (OKRs) and goal governance framework for **TeachLink Web (`Webrinafcode/teachLink_web`)**. Establishing structured goal-setting ensures transparent alignment across engineering, product, and community initiatives.

---

## 1. Goal Setting Methodology
- **Collaborative Formulation**: OKRs are established at the start of each quarter through joint consultation between core maintainers, contributors, and active stakeholders.
- **Alignment**: Objectives must directly support the project's long-term roadmap, architectural hardening, and community growth targets. Each Objective must be accompanied by 3-5 measurable, quantitative Key Results.

---

## 2. Review Cadence
- **Bi-Weekly Check-ins**: Progress against active Key Results is reviewed briefly during bi-weekly maintainer syncs.
- **Quarterly Evaluation**: At the close of each quarter, final scoring (0.0 to 1.0 scale) is performed, analyzing successes, blockers, and lessons learned.

---

## 3. Progress Reporting
- **Public Dashboard**: Status updates and scoring summaries are published openly within project reporting channels and mirrored in repository documentation for community visibility.
