# Retrospective Cadence Policy

## Overview
This document establishes the official retrospective cadence policy for **TeachLink Webrinafcode/teachLink_web** contributors and maintainers to ensure continuous improvement, transparent feedback loops, and accountability.

---

## 1. Cadence Schedule
- **Sprint Retrospectives**: Held bi-weekly on the final Friday of each completed sprint cycle (16:00 UTC).
- **Release Retrospectives**: Held asynchronously or within 48 hours following major version releases or milestone deployments.

---

## 2. Format & Agenda
Retrospectives follow the standard "Start, Stop, Continue" format, structured as follows:
1. **Review Action Items (10 mins)**: Review status of action items assigned during the previous retrospective.
2. **Collect Feedback (15 mins)**: Team members independently log points under three categories:
   - **Start**: New practices, tools, or workflows to adopt.
   - **Stop**: Bottlenecks, inefficient processes, or tech debt to eliminate.
   - **Continue**: Successful workflows and positive team dynamics to maintain.
3. **Group & Vote (10 mins)**: Cluster similar points and vote on priority topics for discussion.
4. **Action Planning (15 mins)**: Assign concrete owners and due dates for items requiring resolution.

---

## 3. Action Item Tracking & Accountability
- **Repository**: All retrospective action items must be logged as tracked issues within the project issue tracker labelled with `retro-action`.
- **Assignment**: Every action item must have a designated DRI (Directly Responsible Individual).
- **Follow-up**: Action item status is reviewed at the start of every subsequent bi-weekly retrospective.
