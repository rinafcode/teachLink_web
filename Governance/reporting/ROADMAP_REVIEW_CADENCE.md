# Roadmap Review Cadence for TeachLink

## Overview
This document defines the official roadmap review frequency, stakeholder participants, required inputs, and governance approval workflow for **TeachLink Web (`Webrinafcode/teachLink_web`)**. Maintaining a structured review cadence ensures transparent product evolution and alignment with community and institutional goals.

---

## 1. Review Frequency & Cadence
- **Bi-Weekly Sync**: Core maintainers and engineering leads conduct a bi-weekly tactical check-in to track sprint velocity and milestone completion.
- **Quarterly Strategic Review**: A comprehensive strategic roadmap evaluation is performed at the end of every quarter to adjust major milestones, resource allocation, and long-term deliverables.

---

## 2. Inputs and Participants

### Participants
- **Core Maintainers & Engineering Leads**: Responsible for technical feasibility and architectural scope.
- **Product & Community Stakeholders**: Representing educator, student, and contributor feedback.

### Required Inputs
- **Velocity & Completion Metrics**: Current progress reports against active milestone deliverables.
- **Community & User Feedback**: Feature requests, bug trends, and support tickets from TeachLink users.
- **Dependency & Infrastructure Updates**: Upcoming technical debt items or breaking external dependencies.

---

## 3. Roadmap Change Approval Process
1. **Proposal Submission**: Proposed additions, removals, or schedule adjustments to the roadmap must be submitted as an issue or RFC (Request for Comments) with the `roadmap-proposal` label.
2. **Review & Discussion**: Proposals are open for community and maintainer feedback for a minimum of 7 calendar days.
3. **Approval Threshold**: Major strategic roadmap changes require consensus approval from core maintainers during the bi-weekly or quarterly review session before being merged into the official project roadmap.
