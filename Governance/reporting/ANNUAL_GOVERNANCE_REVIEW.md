# Annual Governance Review Policy

## Overview
This policy establishes the formal process for conducting the annual governance review for **TeachLink Webrinafcode/teachLink_web**. The review ensures that governance structures, contributor policies, maintainer roles, and reporting mechanisms remain aligned with project goals, community growth, and industry best practices.

---

## 1. Review Scope
The annual governance review encompasses a comprehensive evaluation of:
- **Governance Framework & Policies**: Audit of all documents within the `Governance/` directory (e.g., retrospective cadence, metrics dashboard governance, state-of-the-project reports).
- **Maintainer & Contributor Roles**: Review of active maintainer statuses, voting procedures, access permissions, and leadership succession paths.
- **Reporting & Compliance**: Verification of compliance with reporting cadences, security policies, and code of conduct enforcement.
- **Community & Diversity Health**: Assessment of community onboarding, contributor retention, and inclusive governance initiatives.

---

## 2. Review Participants
- **Lead Facilitators**: Core Project Maintainers and designated Governance Leads.
- **Participants**: Active Maintainers, Key Contributors, and Advisory Board representatives.
- **Community Input**: Open feedback period provided to all community members via public GitHub Discussions prior to finalization.

---

## 3. Required Outputs & Deliverables
Upon completion of the annual review, the following artifacts must be produced and published:
1. **Governance Audit Report**: A published summary detailing policy updates, retired practices, and adopted changes.
2. **Pull Requests for Policy Updates**: Direct updates to relevant files in `Governance/` reflecting agreed-upon changes.
3. **Annual Governance Summary Discussion**: A public GitHub Discussion summarizing key outcomes and community action items for the upcoming year.
