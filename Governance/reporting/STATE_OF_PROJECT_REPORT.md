# State-of-the-Project Report Policy

## Overview
This policy establishes the official guidelines for publishing periodic State-of-the-Project reports for **TeachLink Webrinafcode/teachLink_web**. These reports provide stakeholders, maintainers, and contributors with a transparent overview of project health, milestone progress, and strategic direction.

---

## 1. Cadence Schedule
- **Frequency**: Published **quarterly** (at the conclusion of each fiscal quarter: March, June, September, December).
- **Owner**: Project lead or designated release manager.

---

## 2. Standard Report Sections
Every State-of-the-Project report must include the following sections:
1. **Executive Summary**: A high-level synthesis of project momentum, major achievements, and overarching challenges during the quarter.
2. **Key Milestones & Deliverables**: Review of features shipped, architectural upgrades completed, and governance updates implemented.
3. **Project Metrics & Health**: Quantitative indicators including:
   - PR velocity and average merge time.
   - Issue backlog and closure rate.
   - Contributor growth and community engagement statistics.
4. **Upcoming Focus Areas & Roadmap**: Forward-looking priorities, upcoming epics, and planned architectural milestones for the next quarter.
5. **Risks & Blockers**: Transparent disclosure of technical debt, resource constraints, or external dependencies requiring community or maintainer attention.

---

## 3. Publication Location & Archiving
- **Primary Publication**: Published as a GitHub Discussion under the `Announcements` category and mirrored in the project release notes.
- **Archival**: Permanent copies must be archived under the `Governance/reporting/archives/` directory for historical reference and governance auditing.
