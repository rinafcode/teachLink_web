# Whistleblower Policy for TeachLink

## Overview
This policy establishes a secure and confidential mechanism for contributors, maintainers, community members, and users of **TeachLink Web (`Webrinafcode/teachLink_web`)** to report ethical violations, security breaches, harassment, fraud, or misconduct without fear of reprisal.

---

## 1. Reporting Channels
Reports can be submitted through the following designated channels:
- **Confidential Email Reporting**: Submissions may be sent directly to the designated compliance liaison at `compliance@teachlink-project.org`.
- **Anonymous Reporting Form**: An encrypted, zero-knowledge reporting portal is accessible via the project's security and governance landing page.
- **Direct Escalation**: Reports may also be escalated privately to any core maintainer who is not implicated in the reported incident.

---

## 2. Non-Retaliation Guarantee
- **Zero Tolerance for Retaliation**: TeachLink maintains a strict **zero-retaliation** policy. No contributor, maintainer, or community member who reports a good-faith concern of misconduct shall suffer demotion, suspension, expulsion, harassment, or professional disadvantage.
- **Confidentiality**: All reports, investigative notes, and whistleblower identities will be kept strictly confidential to the fullest extent possible under applicable operational standards.

---

## 3. Investigation Process
1. **Initial Triage**: The compliance committee reviews incoming reports within 48 hours to assess credibility and severity.
2. **Independent Inquiry**: An impartial investigator (or designated uninvolved maintainer) gathers relevant evidence, logs, and testimony.
3. **Resolution & Remediation**: Findings are documented, and appropriate corrective actions (ranging from code removal to contributor banishment) are executed promptly.

---

## 4. Ownership & Review Cadence
- **Primary Owner**: TeachLink Governance & Ethics Working Group.
- **Review Cadence**: Audited annually.
