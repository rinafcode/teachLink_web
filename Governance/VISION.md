# TeachLink Web Vision

TeachLink Web aims to become a trusted, open learning network where useful
knowledge moves freely between people, communities, and opportunities. A learner
should be able to find credible guidance, understand its context, and use it to
make progress. A teacher or practitioner should be able to share experience,
build reputation through contribution, and reach people who can benefit from it.

## Intended End State

- Learning content is easy to find, compare, understand, and revisit.
- Contributors receive clear attribution and meaningful ways to participate.
- Accessibility, privacy, safety, and reliability are treated as product
  requirements rather than afterthoughts.
- Community feedback and evidence guide the evolution of the platform.
- The project remains maintainable through clear ownership, documented decisions,
  and a healthy contributor pathway.

## Guiding Principles

1. **Useful over noisy:** prioritize clarity, practical value, and durable learning
   over activity for its own sake.
2. **Open with care:** encourage sharing while respecting privacy, consent,
   attribution, and safety.
3. **Accessible by default:** design for different abilities, devices, contexts,
   and levels of technical access.
4. **Evidence before assumptions:** use user feedback, observed outcomes, and
   transparent reasoning to guide important changes.
5. **Build for continuity:** prefer solutions that contributors can understand,
   operate, and improve over time.
