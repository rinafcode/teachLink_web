# TeachLink Web Mission

TeachLink Web exists to help people learn from one another by making knowledge,
skills, ideas, and useful information easier to share, discover, and apply. We
build an inclusive platform where contributors can turn expertise into practical
value and where learners can find resources that support personal and community
development.

## Measurable Outcomes

- People can publish and discover useful learning content with clear ownership and
  attribution.
- Core learning workflows remain understandable and accessible across supported
  devices.
- The platform earns sustained participation from both people sharing knowledge
  and people applying it.
- Contributors can improve the project through a transparent, documented process.

Every significant feature should explain which of these outcomes it advances and
how its impact will be observed after release.
