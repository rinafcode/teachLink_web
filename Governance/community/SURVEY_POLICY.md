# Community Survey Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how TeachLink Web runs surveys of its community: how they
are designed and announced, how responses are kept private, and how results are
shared. Done carefully, surveys help the project listen to contributors and
users without burdening them or misusing their data.

## Scope

This policy applies to surveys, polls, and questionnaires run by or on behalf of
the project that collect responses from contributors, users, or the wider
community. Data collection inside the product is covered by the [Analytics Data
Collection](../domains/ANALYTICS_DATA.md) and the [Privacy Notice
governance](../domains/PRIVACY_NOTICE.md).

## How Surveys Are Run

Each survey follows a short process:

- **Proposal.** A maintainer or contributor opens an issue stating the goal, the
  questions, the audience, the duration, and how the results will be used. Two
  maintainers approve it.
- **Design.** Questions are as few as needed, neutral, and inclusive (see the
  community inclusive language guide), and each is optional unless essential.
  Demographic questions are optional and justified.
- **Announcement.** The survey is announced in the project's official channels
  with the purpose, how long it takes, who will see the responses, and the
  closing date. No more than one general community survey runs at a time and
  none runs more often than once every 6 months, so people are not
  over-surveyed.
- **Consent.** Taking part is voluntary; the survey says so and links to the
  data handling statement. Children are not surveyed without the safeguards in
  the children's data policy.
- **Tools.** Only tools approved under the vendor risk management policy are
  used.

## Privacy of Responses

Respondents are protected:

- Surveys are anonymous by default. If identification is needed (for example to
  follow up), the survey says why, and the identifying data is stored separately
  and deleted when it is no longer required.
- Only the survey owner and the maintainers named in the proposal can see raw
  responses; free-text answers are reviewed for personal data before anything is
  quoted.
- Raw responses are kept for no longer than the stated period, and then deleted
  according to the record retention and legal hold policy.
- Results are never used to identify, rank, or discipline individuals, and
  responses are not shared with sponsors or third parties.
- Where a respondent can be identified, the project applies the privacy and
  erasure principles in the [Privacy Notice
  governance](../domains/PRIVACY_NOTICE.md).

## Sharing Results

The community sees what it contributed to:

- A summary of results is published within 60 days of the survey closing, in the
  same channels used to announce it.
- Results are reported in aggregate; groups smaller than 5 are merged or
  withheld so nobody can be identified.
- The summary states the number of respondents, the response rate, the limits of
  the sample, and the decisions or follow-up actions the results informed.
- Open-ended answers are summarised or lightly edited with consent; quotes never
  reveal identity.
- Where it is safe to do so, anonymised data is published so others can check
  the findings.

## Ownership and Review

- Maintainers own this document and are accountable for applying it
  consistently.
- The survey owner is responsible for each step in the survey's life cycle, from
  approval to deletion of raw data.
- Changes are proposed in a pull request that touches only the `Governance/`
  folder and are reviewed like any other governance change (see the [Review
  Policy](../policies/REVIEW_POLICY.md)).
- This document is versioned with the repository and describes how the project
  actually operates rather than an aspiration.

## Success

This policy succeeds when surveys are rare, short, and purposeful, when
respondents trust that their answers are private, and when the community sees
what changed because of what it said.
