# Press and Media Policy

## Purpose

This policy says who may speak to journalists, researchers, and other media on
behalf of TeachLink Web, how statements are approved, and what messages are
consistent with the project. It protects contributors from being misquoted or
pressured and makes the project's public statements accurate and coordinated.

## Scope

This policy applies to interviews, press releases, quotes, podcasts, conference
press, and other communications with media or analysts that concern the project.
It is read together with the social media policy
([SOCIAL_MEDIA.md](SOCIAL_MEDIA.md)) and the use of project names and logos
described in the [Brand Usage](../BRAND_USAGE.md).

## Who Speaks to Media

Spokespeople are limited so statements are consistent:

- The lead maintainers, or maintainers they designate for a topic, may speak for
  the project.
- Any other contributor who is approached by media says they speak for
  themselves, not for the project, unless a maintainer has agreed otherwise in
  writing.
- Anyone approached directly forwards the request to the maintainers through the
  project's designated contact within 2 business days, without committing to an
  interview or deadline.
- Spokespeople never share private information about contributors, users,
  security matters, or conduct cases (see the [Security
  Policy](../SECURITY_POLICY.md) and [Code of Conduct
  reporting](../COC_REPORTING.md)).

## Approval Process

Statements are approved before they are released:

- A maintainer acknowledges each request, notes the outlet, topic, deadline, and
  what is being asked, and records it.
- For written statements and press releases, two maintainers review the text for
  accuracy, privacy, and consistency with published project decisions, and
  approve it in the request thread.
- For live or recorded interviews, the spokesperson is briefed on the topics,
  the facts that may be shared, and the matters that must not be discussed, and
  may ask to review quotes before publication.
- Urgent statements about security incidents follow the [Security
  Policy](../SECURITY_POLICY.md) and may be approved by one maintainer with the
  second maintainer informed immediately afterwards.
- Approved statements are published on the project's official channels so the
  public record is complete.

## Messaging Guidance

Messages are factual and in line with the project's purpose:

- Describe the project using the [Mission](../MISSION.md) and
  [Vision](../VISION.md); do not claim endorsements, adoption numbers, or
  roadmap dates that are not published.
- Distinguish clearly between what is released, what is planned, and what is an
  opinion of the speaker.
- Be neutral about other projects and vendors, and disclose any affiliation or
  sponsorship (see the [Conflict of Interest
  policy](../policies/CONFLICT_OF_INTEREST.md)).
- Do not speculate about incidents under investigation; say what is known, what
  is being done, and when the next update is expected.
- Acknowledge contributors accurately and with their consent, and correct errors
  in coverage by contacting the outlet politely and recording the correction.

## Ownership and Review

- Maintainers own this document and are accountable for applying it
  consistently.
- Maintainers keep the request log and the list of designated spokespeople.
- Changes are proposed in a pull request that touches only the `Governance/`
  folder and are reviewed like any other governance change (see the [Review
  Policy](../policies/REVIEW_POLICY.md)).
- This document is versioned with the repository and describes how the project
  actually operates rather than an aspiration.

## Success

This policy succeeds when the project's public statements are accurate and
consistent, when contributors are not pulled into speaking for the project
against their will, and when every statement can be traced to an approval.
