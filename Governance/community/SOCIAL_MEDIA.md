# Social Media Policy

## Purpose

This policy sets rules for the project's official social media presence and for
how contributors talk about TeachLink Web on social media. It keeps the
project's public voice consistent, respectful, and honest, and makes clear when
someone speaks for the project and when they speak for themselves.

## Scope

This policy applies to the project's official accounts and to contributors who
identify themselves as connected with the project when posting. Statements to
journalists and formal public statements are covered by the press and media
policy in this folder ([MEDIA_POLICY.md](MEDIA_POLICY.md)). Personal accounts
are otherwise the individual's own; this policy asks only for the courtesy of
clarity.

## Who May Post Officially

Official accounts speak for the project, so access is limited:

- Only maintainers, and contributors the maintainers have designated in writing
  as social media stewards, may post from official accounts.
- Stewards post about releases, events, community news, and recognised
  contributions. They do not announce policy changes, roadmap commitments, or
  security matters that have not been published through the proper process.
- Access to accounts follows least privilege, uses strong authentication, and is
  reviewed on the cadence in the access review policy; credentials are never
  shared in chat or issues.
- A steward who leaves the role hands over the account and has their access
  removed the same day.

## Tone and Disclosure

Posts are written to reflect the [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and
the project's [values](../VALUES.md):

- Be accurate, courteous, and inclusive (see the [Communication
  Norms](../policies/COMMUNICATION_NORMS.md)); correct mistakes openly and
  promptly rather than deleting them.
- Do not argue with critics, disparage other projects or people, or post about
  private conduct cases or security details.
- Credit contributors and sources, and obtain consent before sharing a person's
  words, image, or personal information.
- Disclose relationships: a contributor who posts about the project while paid
  by or affiliated with a vendor says so. Paid promotion and sponsored content
  are labelled as such.
- Contributors posting from personal accounts should make clear they speak for
  themselves and not for the project, unless they are acting under the [Brand
  Usage](../BRAND_USAGE.md) and the press and media policy.

## Escalation

When something goes wrong:

- A post that is wrong, harmful, or exposes private information is taken down or
  corrected immediately by a steward, and a maintainer is told.
- Harassment, threats, or abuse aimed at the project or its contributors are not
  answered in kind; they are recorded, reported to the platform where
  appropriate, and handled through [Code of Conduct
  reporting](../COC_REPORTING.md) when a contributor is involved.
- Mentions of a possible vulnerability are moved to the private reporting
  channel in the [Security Policy](../SECURITY_POLICY.md) and not discussed
  publicly.
- Media enquiries received on social media are passed to the people named in the
  press and media policy.
- Serious or unclear cases follow the [escalation
  path](../processes/ESCALATION_PATH.md). Lessons from an incident are recorded
  and this policy updated if it fell short.

## Ownership and Review

- Maintainers own this document and are accountable for applying it
  consistently.
- Maintainers keep the list of social media stewards and the inventory of
  official accounts.
- Changes are proposed in a pull request that touches only the `Governance/`
  folder and are reviewed like any other governance change (see the [Review
  Policy](../policies/REVIEW_POLICY.md)).
- This document is versioned with the repository and describes how the project
  actually operates rather than an aspiration.

## Success

This policy succeeds when the project's public voice is consistent and
trustworthy, when it is clear who speaks for the project, and when mistakes are
corrected quickly and in the open.
