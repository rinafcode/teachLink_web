# Donation and Sponsorship Policy

## Purpose

This policy sets out how TeachLink Web accepts donations and sponsorship, what
conflicts of interest must be avoided, and what the project commits to make
public. It lets supporters help the project while keeping its decisions
independent and its finances open.

## Scope

This policy applies to money, goods, services, and in-kind support offered to
the project or to events held in its name, whether from individuals, companies,
or other organisations. Management of project funds falls to the [Treasurer
role](../roles/TREASURER.md), and sponsorship of community events also follows
the community events policy in this folder (`EVENTS_POLICY.md`) where it exists.

## Accepted Sponsorship Forms

The project may accept:

- **Cash donations** from individuals and organisations, one-off or recurring,
  through the channels listed in the repository.
- **Sponsorship** of infrastructure, tooling, events, or contributor time, where
  the support is described in a written, public agreement.
- **In-kind support**, such as hosting credits, licences, or services, when the
  terms are recorded and do not restrict the project's choices.
- The project does not accept support that is conditional on: control of the
  roadmap or technical decisions, exclusivity, access to user data, changes to
  review or release outcomes, or silence about a sponsor's conduct.
- Sponsors from sectors or jurisdictions that conflict with the [Code of
  Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) or the project's legal obligations may be
  declined.

## Conflict-of-Interest Rules

Funding must not become influence:

- Sponsorship gives recognition only, not a vote, a maintainer seat, or priority
  for issues or pull requests.
- A maintainer or contributor who is employed by, or has a financial
  relationship with, a sponsor declares it and steps out of decisions that
  directly concern that sponsor, as required by the [Conflict of Interest
  policy](../policies/CONFLICT_OF_INTEREST.md).
- Sponsor-funded work is proposed, reviewed, and merged under the same rules as
  any other change (see the [Review Policy](../policies/REVIEW_POLICY.md)).
- Gifts to individual contributors from sponsors are disclosed to the
  maintainers and are not accepted if they could reasonably appear to affect a
  decision.
- The project does not endorse a sponsor's products; sponsor acknowledgement
  follows the [Brand Usage](../BRAND_USAGE.md).

## Transparency Commitments

The project publishes:

- a public list of sponsors and the amounts or categories of support received,
  unless a donor asks to be anonymous and the maintainers agree;
- a periodic summary of income and spending by category, prepared by the
  [Treasurer role](../roles/TREASURER.md) and included in the [Transparency
  Reports](../policies/TRANSPARENCY_REPORTS.md);
- the terms under which each significant sponsorship was accepted, with personal
  data removed;
- any declined offer of significant size and the reason, when this can be said
  without harming the donor.
- Records of funds are kept for the periods set out in the record retention and
  legal hold policy and are available to maintainers for audit.

## Ownership and Review

- Maintainers own this document and are accountable for applying it
  consistently.
- The treasurer reviews sponsorship records each quarter and reports any term
  that conflicts with this policy.
- Changes are proposed in a pull request that touches only the `Governance/`
  folder and are reviewed like any other governance change (see the [Review
  Policy](../policies/REVIEW_POLICY.md)).
- This document is versioned with the repository and describes how the project
  actually operates rather than an aspiration.

## Success

This policy succeeds when supporters can help with clear expectations, when no
sponsor gains influence over project decisions, and when anyone can see where
the project's support comes from and how it is used.
