# Contributor Recognition Program

This document outlines the contributor recognition program for TeachLink Web, including recognition tiers, criteria, and the nomination process.

## Recognition Tiers

We value all contributions and have established the following tiers to recognize our contributors:

- **Bronze Contributor:** For regular contributors who have made a number of valuable contributions.
- **Silver Contributor:** For contributors who have shown a sustained commitment to the project and have taken on more responsibility.
- **Gold Contributor:** For contributors who have demonstrated leadership and have had a significant impact on the project.

## Criteria for Each Tier

- **Bronze:** At least 10 merged pull requests.
- **Silver:** At least 25 merged pull requests and active participation in discussions.
- **Gold:** Significant contributions to the project's direction, mentorship of other contributors, and a long-term commitment to the project's success.

## Nomination Process

Contributors can be nominated for a higher recognition tier by any existing community member. The project leadership will review nominations on a quarterly basis.
