# TeachLink Meeting Agenda Template

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Scope:** All synchronous core maintainer syncs, architectural review calls, and working group meetings across TeachLink projects.

---

## 1. Purpose
To ensure productive, time-efficient, and well-documented meetings, this template establishes standardized agenda sections, time-boxing guidelines, and item submission rules for all synchronous sessions.

---

## 2. Agenda Item Submission Rules
* **Deadline:** All agenda items must be proposed and added to the collaborative working document at least **24 hours prior** to the scheduled meeting start time.
* **Format:** Submissions must include a descriptive title, the presenting contributor's username, estimated discussion duration, and links to relevant GitHub issues, pull requests, or architectural RFCs.
* **Priority:** Urgent security incidents or blocking architectural deadlocks take precedence over routine backlog reviews.

---

## 3. Standard Meeting Agenda Structure

```markdown
# Meeting: [Core Maintainer Sync / Working Group Name] - YYYY-MM-DD
**Host / Facilitator:** [Name]  
**Note-Taker:** [Name]  
**Recording Link:** [URL or "Pending"]  

---

## 1. Opening & Attendance (5 Mins)
- Roll call of participating maintainers.
- Review and approval of previous meeting minutes.

## 2. Urgent / Blocking Items (15 Mins)
*Time-Boxed: Max 5 minutes per item unless extended by majority consent.*
- [Item 1 Title & Link] (Presenter: @handle) - [Est. Time]
- [Item 2 Title & Link] (Presenter: @handle) - [Est. Time]

## 3. Architectural & Proposal Reviews (25 Mins)
- [RFC / Proposal Title & Link] (Presenter: @handle) - [Est. Time]

## 4. Open Floor & Community Q&A (10 Mins)
- Ad-hoc discussion items raised by attending contributors.

## 5. Action Item Summary & Next Steps (5 Mins)
- Review action items, assign owners, and confirm target delivery dates.
