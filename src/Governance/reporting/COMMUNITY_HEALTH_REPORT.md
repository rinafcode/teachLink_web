# Community Health Report Policy for TeachLink

## Overview
This document defines the official community health reporting policy for **TeachLink Web (`Webrinafcode/teachLink_web`)**. Monitoring and reporting on community engagement, contributor retention, review responsiveness, and inclusivity ensures a thriving, sustainable open-source ecosystem.

---

## 1. Community Health Metrics
Each community health report must evaluate and publish the following core indicators:
- **Contributor Growth & Retention**: Number of first-time contributors, active monthly maintainers, and returning contributors.
- **Review Latency & Throughput**: Average time taken for pull requests to receive initial review feedback and final merge times.
- **Issue Resolution Speed**: Average time elapsed between issue creation and closure or triage.
- **Inclusivity & Support**: Summary of community support channels activity, code of conduct compliance reviews, and mentorship participation.

---

## 2. Reporting Cadence
- **Frequency**: Published **quarterly** (at the end of each calendar quarter, aligning with project review cycles).

---

## 3. Publication Location
- **Repository Destination**: Published within the `Governance/reporting/` directory or project release archives.
- **Community Channels**: Highlighted in project discussions and community newsletters to ensure broad accessibility for contributors and users.
