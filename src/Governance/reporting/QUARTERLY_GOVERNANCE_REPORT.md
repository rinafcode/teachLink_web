# Quarterly Governance Report Policy for TeachLink

## Overview
This document defines the official policy and structure for publishing the **Quarterly Governance Report** for **TeachLink Web (`Webrinafcode/teachLink_web`)**. Transparency in governance, security posture, and contributor activity is essential for maintaining community trust and project accountability.

---

## 1. Report Contents
Each quarterly report must compile and summarize key metrics and operational updates across the following categories:
- **Governance & Policy Updates**: Summary of any new or amended governance documents, runbooks, or compliance policies introduced during the quarter.
- **Security & Vulnerability Status**: Overview of dependency audits, security patches applied, and incident response summaries (without disclosing sensitive exploits).
- **Contribution & Community Health**: Pull request throughput, contributor growth, review latency metrics, and community feedback highlights.
- **Roadmap Milestones**: Progress tracking against active OKRs and quarterly roadmap deliverables.

---

## 2. Publication Cadence
- **Timing**: Published within 10 business days following the close of each calendar quarter (by April 15, July 15, October 15, and January 15).
- **Distribution Channel**: Published openly in the repository under the `Governance/reporting/` archive or project release notes, and announced across community communication channels.

---

## 3. Ownership
- **Primary Owner**: TeachLink Core Governance Working Group & Lead Maintainers.
- **Responsibilities**: Drafting, reviewing, and publishing the report in coordination with security and engineering leads.
