# Content Moderation Policy

This document outlines the policy for moderating user-generated content on our platforms.

## Prohibited Content

The following types of content are strictly prohibited:

- Hate speech, harassment, and bullying
- Spam and malicious content
- Nudity and sexually explicit content
- Violence and graphic content
- Misinformation and disinformation
- Illegal activities

## Review Workflow

1.  **Automated Filtering:** User-generated content is first scanned by an automated system to detect and flag prohibited content.
2.  **Manual Review:** Flagged content is then reviewed by a human moderator within 24 hours.
3.  **Action:** If the content is found to violate our policy, it will be removed, and the user may be subject to penalties, including account suspension or termination.

## Appeal Path

Users who believe their content was wrongly removed can submit an appeal within 30 days. The appeal will be reviewed by a different moderator, and a final decision will be communicated to the user.

## Regression Tests

Where applicable, regression tests will be added to ensure that the content moderation system effectively and consistently enforces our policies.
